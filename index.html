Common questions

Where are my trips stored? Only on your phone. Your phone's own backup (such as iCloud Backup) can include them.

How do I delete my data? In the app, open Settings. "Delete my cloud data" removes any share links you created; the device wipe, or deleting the app, removes trips and photos from your phone.

How do I stop a share link? Open the trip's Share dialog and tap Stop sharing. The link stops working right away.
