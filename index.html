Pick a handful of screenshots at once, like a café's post, a museum's opening hours or a friend's text, then tap Done. TripWright reads the text with Apple's Vision on your iPhone and turns each one into an activity.

  • Choose many screenshots in one go.

Common questions

Where are my trips stored?

Only on your phone. Your phone's own backup (such as iCloud Backup) can include them.

Does TripWright use AI?

Only on your iPhone. "Build my trip" uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on the device; nothing you type is sent to an AI company. Reading bookings and screenshots also happens on the phone.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings. "Delete my cloud data" removes any share links you created; the device wipe, or deleting the app, removes trips and photos from your phone.

How do I stop a share link?

Open the trip's Share dialog and tap Stop sharing. The link stops working right away.

When can I download it?

TripWright is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. Email us if you'd like a note when it's out.
