Privacy Policy

TripWright · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Overview

TripWright keeps your trips and photos on your phone. They are not uploaded to our servers unless you create a share link, and photos are never uploaded at all. There are no accounts. This policy describes what is stored, which other services receive data, and how to delete it.

1. Information We Collect

a) Trip data you create

Trips, destinations, dates, day notes, activities (titles, times, addresses, descriptions), confirmation codes, and photos are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. There is no cloud backup by TripWright. Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud Backup) may include the app's data, under your phone provider's terms. If you delete the app without a phone backup, your trips and photos are gone.

b) Device identifier

The app generates a random device ID and keeps it on your device. It is sent to our backend only with share link and destination search requests, for rate limiting and to manage links you created. It is not linked to your name, email, or any account.

c) Photos

Activity photos you add are compressed and stored on your device only. They are never uploaded to our backend and are never included in share links. You can send them yourself with the share sheet (for example AirDrop, Messages, or a shared photo album); the app you pick handles them under its own policy.

d) No cloud AI, and imports stay on your device

TripWright does not send anything you enter to an AI provider. “Build my trip” uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone; what you type there stays on the device. If you choose to copy its prompt into a chatbot yourself, that chatbot’s own privacy policy applies. When you import a document, spreadsheet or booking email, the app reads it on your device with fixed rules; the file and its text are not uploaded.

e) Location, weather, and maps

We do not request your device's GPS. When you type a destination or an address, your search text is sent directly from your device to Photon (photon.komoot.io), an OpenStreetMap search service run by komoot, which can see your device's IP address and what you searched for. The same service is used to place your trip's destinations on the map. To show weather, the app sends the destination name directly from your device to Open-Meteo's geocoding service, then sends the resulting coordinates and your trip dates to Open-Meteo's forecast service. The map panel loads map data and fonts from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which can see your device's IP address and which map areas you view. Your choice of map language is stored only on your device.

f) Crash and error data

The app does not send crash reports to third parties in the current release. Basic diagnostic information may be written to your device's console.

g) What we do NOT collect

  • We do not require an account, name, or email.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles about you.

2. Third-Party Services

These services receive data as described. Services loaded directly by your device can see your IP address.

  • Supabase: hosts the database and backend functions for share links, collaboration, destination search, and exchange rates. It does not store your photos.
  • Open-Meteo: weather forecasts; receives the destination name and coordinates directly from your device. Terms.
  • Photon (komoot): destination and address search, and placing destinations on the map (directly from your device).
  • OpenFreeMap: map data, map fonts and map icons (directly from your device).
  • Exchange rates: rates come from open.er-api.com, fetched by our backend on a schedule. The currency calculator reads them from our database, so nothing about you is sent to that source.
  • Google Fonts: the app's fonts may be loaded from Google.
  • Unsplash: sample trips display images loaded from Unsplash.

Links you tap (maps, translation, travel apps, photo albums) open in another app or your browser, which then handles your data under its own policy.

This web page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record the IP address of visitors to this page under its own privacy statement; it does not receive any data from the app.

3. Sharing and Collaboration

If you generate a share link, a copy of the trip is stored on our backend and a URL is created that lets anyone with the link view it, or, if you choose "Can view and edit", change any part of it. Anyone who has the link can open it. Photos are never part of a link. Confirmation codes are left out unless you turn on "Include confirmation codes" for that link. Edits made through a "Can view and edit" link are copied back into the trip on your phone. View-only links expire 6 months after the trip's end date (collaborative links after at most 90 days). An expired link no longer shows the trip, and its data is deleted by cleanup 30 days later. You can turn a link off at any time with Stop sharing in the Share dialog; the link stops working immediately. Shared links never include the keys that let you edit or delete them.

You can also send a trip as a file, with or without its photos, using AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or Files. The file goes straight from your phone to the person you choose and does not pass through our backend. Anyone who receives it can open it in TripWright and gets their own copy.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Trip data and photos stay on your device until you delete them. Copies on our backend exist only for share links you created (and, for devices that used older versions of the app, earlier cloud backups). You can:

  • Delete a single trip from the trip menu. It is removed from your device immediately, together with photos that no other trip uses. Use Stop sharing first if you also want its share links removed right away.
  • Use Delete my cloud data in Settings to remove everything on our backend tied to your device ID (shared links, and any backups or photos from older versions) right away.
  • Use the device-only wipe in Settings, or delete the app, to clear your trips and photos from this device. This does not remove share links by itself.
  • Email aaproducts8212+tripwright@gmail.com with a deletion request.

Backups and photos stored by older versions of the app are deleted by scheduled cleanup once a device has not synced for 90 days. Server logs are kept under the hosting platform's log retention.

5. Children's Privacy

TripWright is not directed to children. You must be 13 or older to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will address it promptly.
